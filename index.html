When something changes on the plant floor — a vendor session, a firmware update, a configuration edit — GoLeap works out whether it was supposed to happen, whether the equipment is still behaving as it should, what production depends on it, and who needs to act.

Three questions after every change.

Detection tools tell a plant that something changed. Deciding what it means still takes days, several people, and a meeting. GoLeap answers the questions that meeting exists to answer.

Was it supposed to happen?

The observed change, matched against the work that was approved and the access that was granted — by whom, when, and within what scope.

Is the equipment still right?

How the asset behaves after the change, compared with how it behaved before on the same work. Approved work can still leave a machine abnormal.

What is at risk, and who acts?

Which orders depend on this asset, what else can make them, and the bounded next step routed to the person accountable for it.

Works with what a plant already runs.

GoLeap reads the records your maintenance, access, monitoring, and operating systems already produce, and brings them into one reviewable investigation. It does not replace those systems.

  • Nothing is installed on controllers or production equipment.
  • Network observation is passive. GoLeap never sends commands to a machine.
  • Raw plant data stays under the plant's control; only what is needed for a decision moves.
  • Actions are proposals until a named person approves them, and every approval is recorded with its evidence.
  • What is missing is stated, not guessed.

Built for plants where a wrong call is expensive.

Manufacturers running mixed-OEM equipment, with vendors who need remote access, maintenance and security teams that rarely see the same evidence, and downtime that is measured in orders rather than hours.
