Our mission

Turn fragmented signals into operational understanding.

Industrial environments already generate enormous amounts of data. But the evidence needed to understand a critical asset is split across security, maintenance, engineering, identity, vendor, and operating systems.

GoLeap’s mission is to help industrial teams understand what changed, why it matters, what may be at risk, and what should happen next—with the evidence and uncertainty visible.

The concept

From evidence to governed action.

GoLeap is designed as an intelligence and coordination layer—not another system of record. It connects the tools a plant already runs and adds cross-domain reasoning around the assets that matter.

01 / CONNECT

Build shared context

Correlate asset state, condition, maintenance, software, access, vendor, and cyber evidence around one operational picture.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Reason about consequence

Assess change and risk in the context of how an asset behaves, what operations depend on it, and what evidence is still missing.

03 / GOVERN

Coordinate the next step

Notify the right human or invoke a bounded workflow through existing systems—with approvals, constraints, and an auditable record.

Illustrative scenario

A machine fault. A recent software change.

A hypothetical example of the intended concept.

Imagine a CNC machine starts faulting shortly after a software update. Is the change responsible, or could wear or a maintenance issue explain the fault?

GoLeap could bring fault signals, the software change record, maintenance history, and access events into one investigation—helping an engineer test possible causes without treating timing alone as proof.

The next step: the engineer could review the evidence and approve a diagnostic workflow through existing tools before any production change.

Designed for industrial reality

Intelligence the plant can trust.

Industrial environments are safety-sensitive, heterogeneous, and operationally constrained. Those conditions shape the concept from the start.

Agentless at the asset

Work from passive and approved data sources without placing new software on controllers or production equipment.

Evidence before inference

Preserve provenance, surface uncertainty, and separate what is observed from what the system concludes.

Governed by the plant

Keep people, policy, and site-specific operating boundaries in control of any consequential action.

Designed to integrate

Add intelligence across existing OT, security, maintenance, identity, and enterprise systems rather than asking teams to replace them.
